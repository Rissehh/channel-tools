Data deletion

Last updated October 27, 2026.

Riss Channel Tools is a private app used only by its owner on his own accounts. It stores sign-in tokens and basic account details (Page and account names and ids) on the owner's own computers, and nothing else from Facebook or Instagram.

To delete your data

  1. Remove the app in Facebook: Settings and privacy, Settings, Apps and Websites, select Riss Channel Tools, Remove. This revokes its access immediately.
  2. Or email the developer contact address shown on the app's Facebook sign-in screen and ask for deletion. All tokens and stored details for your account are deleted within 30 days and the deletion is confirmed by email.
