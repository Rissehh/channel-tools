Privacy policy

Last updated October 27, 2026.

What this app is

Riss Channel Tools is a private app used by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels and their matching TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts. It is not offered to other people.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channels, read those channels' public statistics, videos and comments, and post replies as those channels.

With the owner's permission through Facebook Login, the app uses the Meta Graph API to publish the owner's own videos as Reels to his own Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts, and to read those Pages' and accounts' basic details (name, id) to confirm it is posting to the right one. On TikTok, the owner's videos are uploaded to his own accounts through TikTok's website while he is signed in.

What it stores

Sign-in tokens, channel statistics and comment text are stored only on the owner's own computers. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

How to remove access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in Facebook's Settings, Apps and Websites (Meta). Stored data is deleted when the owner removes the app's files. See data deletion.

Google API data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions go to the developer contact email shown on the Google or Facebook sign-in consent screen for this app.
